MYGA

Sky Harbourview Multi-Year Guaranteed Annuity

A guaranteed rate of return over a set period of time — with full principal protection, tax-deferred growth, and a death benefit for your beneficiaries.

Ready to learn more? Our team can walk you through the right strategy.

Why Sky Harbourview MYGA

What your contract provides

Six core protections and benefits built into every Sky Harbourview MYGA contract.

Key Features

Principal Protection: Your premium is 100% protected. Market downturns cannot reduce your contract value — your floor is zero loss.

Guaranteed Interest Rate: Your declared interest rate is set at policy issue and locked for the entire length of your chosen guarantee period.

Tax-Deferred Earnings: Interest compounds tax-deferred until you choose to access funds, allowing for more efficient long-term accumulation.

Access to Funds: After the first 12 months, access up to 10% of your account value each year without surrender charges.

Death Benefit for Beneficiaries: Full contract value (no MVA or surrender charges) is paid to your named beneficiaries at death. Spousal continuation option is available.

Flexible Settlement Options: At the end of your term, choose to renew, withdraw, or convert to an income stream for life or a specified period.

Riders

Built-in benefit provisions

The following riders are included at no additional charge, activated after the first contract anniversary.

Nursing Home Confinement No Charge

After the first contract anniversary, if the contract owner is confined to a nursing home for at least 90 consecutive days (or 90 days with no more than a 6-month break), any applicable MVA or surrender charges are waived on any withdrawal. Confinement must be medically necessary and prescribed by a qualified physician.

Terminal Illness No Charge

After the first contract anniversary, if the contract owner is terminally ill and not expected to live more than 12 months, any applicable MVA and surrender charges are waived on any withdrawal. Terminal illness must be diagnosed by a qualified physician after the contract's issue date.

*Waiver of surrender and MVA charges based on final review of claim.

End of Guarantee Period

Options at the end of your term

When your guarantee period concludes, you have several options available.

Renew for Another Term

Continue earning with a new declared rate for a new guarantee period of your choice. Rates are declared based on then-current market conditions.

Full Withdrawal

Withdraw the full account value without surrender charges or Market Value Adjustment.

Elect an Income Option

Begin receiving income payments through one of the available settlement options.

Spousal Continuation

For jointly owned contracts or a single-owner contract with a sole spouse beneficiary: in the event of death, the surviving spouse may assume all rights to the initial agreement, maintain tax-deferred status, and choose beneficiaries. Subject to certain conditions.

*For most states, Sky Harbourview MYGA Policy Form: ICC19OLASMA. Product features, options, form numbers, and availability may vary by state. This is a brief description meant for informational purposes only. Not individualized investment or financial advice.

Settlement Options

Income payment options

The Sky Harbourview MYGA can provide an income stream for a term of your choosing, including for the rest of your life.

Life Only

Equal monthly payments for the annuitant's remaining lifetime. Payments end with the payment due just before the annuitant's death.

Life with 10-Year Period Certain

Equal monthly payments for the greater of 120 months or the annuitant's remaining lifetime — ensuring at least 10 years of payments regardless of when the annuitant passes.

Joint and Last Survivor

Payments continue during the lifetime of the annuitant and a designated second person. If both pass within 120 monthly periods of payments beginning, the remaining guaranteed payments continue to the beneficiary.

*Once annuity payments have begun, no changes can be made. Product features and availability may vary by state.
