Annuity Glossary

Key terms, defined

Quick definitions of key terms you'll encounter in Oceanview contracts and client conversations.

1035 Exchange

The tax-exempt transfer of an existing annuity policy to a new annuity or life insurance policy, covering the same person.

Accumulation Period

The phase of an annuity in which premium deposits and interest compounds, prior to the payout phase beginning.

Annuitant

The person whose life is measured to determine annuity payments. Often the same as the contract owner.

Beneficiary

The person entitled to receive death benefit proceeds upon the death of the annuitant or contract owner.

Cap Rate

The maximum percent of an index's gain credited to an indexed annuity in a given crediting period.

Fixed Annuity

Annuity with a guaranteed interest rate, no market exposure, and predictable risk-free growth over a defined term.

Free Withdrawal

A provision allowing the contract owner to withdraw a specified percentage of the account value each year without surrender charges.

Market Value Adjustment (MVA)

An adjustment applied to withdrawals taken during the surrender charge period, based on changes in interest rates.

Participation Rate

A percentage of the index gain that the policyholder receives in a fixed indexed annuity crediting strategy.

Required Minimum Distribution

Minimum amount IRA-qualified accounts must withdraw per year per IRS rules, beginning at age 73.

Surrender Charge

A fee applied to early withdrawals that declines over the surrender charge period until it reaches zero.

Tax Deferral

The postponement of income taxes on annuity earnings until funds are withdrawn, allowing greater compounding over time.
